Description ^

Forget league economy and Meta builds.

Challenge yourself by leading the character with skill limitations.

Master the ability to parry enemies, know your limits as a huntress and learn which enemy attacks should not be parried.

Learn to save your charges to fuse a fiery spear empowered with Mahuika’s anger and find the best target in the pack.

Use the advantage of Rake as a Sprint stopper to surprise your foes with an AOE bleed attack and combo your way through parries, disengage, barrage and explosive spear.

Learn to use Whirling Slash to take advantage of blinded foes and extra damage with elemental infusion.

Use crossbows to unleash your anger against rare and unique monsters that bullies you and your ability to die in Act 1. Do not forget to scream each time you unload your bullets.

Backstory ^

Count Geonor's madness led to the destruction of Mahuika's village. She escaped, but many did not. Thus she sworn a bloody and fiery revenge.

After spending years living amongst count Geonor's guards, Mahuika learned how to use crossbows and bucklers to disrupt enemies defenses.

Time passes, the thirst for revenge grows, Count Geonor is only the first of many.

When the window of opportunity opened, Mahuika attempted a failed attack against Geonor. She got captured...

Now Mahuika faces the same destiny as many do in Wraeclast...
